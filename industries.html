01Industries

Seven industries.
One film.

The same tube protects a garment, a seed packet and a gearbox spare — the gauge, the colour and the finish are what change. If your sector is not listed, the specification conversation is identical: tell us the pack, not the part number.

01

Packaging & converting

Converters who buy film by the tonne and turn it into bags, sleeves and pouches on their own sealing lines. Consistency roll to roll is the whole relationship.

Typical uses

  • Bag and pouch making
  • Sealing and cutting lines
  • Contract packing
02

Textiles & garments

Garment, hosiery and made-up protection where the pack has to be clear enough to read the product and tough enough to survive a container.

Typical uses

  • Garment and hosiery packing
  • Fabric roll protection
  • Export consolidation
03

Stationery & printing

File covers, folders, binding and document protection — where sheet roll and clear tubing both have a job, often on the same order.

Typical uses

  • File and folder covers
  • Book and binder covers
  • Document protection
04

Food & FMCG

Fast-moving goods that need a clean, consistent over-wrap at volume, with the documentation to back it up. Food-contact grade is quoted to specification.

Typical uses

  • Dry goods over-wrap
  • Retail multipacks
  • Shelf-ready presentation
05

Pharmaceutical & medical

Secondary packaging and device protection where batch traceability, retained samples and a documented change process are non-negotiable.

Typical uses

  • Secondary packaging
  • Device and kit protection
  • Batch-traceable supply
06

Agriculture & horticulture

Seed, nursery and produce handling — including the fluorescent grades that stay findable outdoors and in low light.

Typical uses

  • Seed and nursery packing
  • Produce handling
  • Field identification
07

Industrial & hardware

Fasteners, components, spares and tooling: colour-coded by grade, protected against handling damage, kitted and identified at the point of pack.

Typical uses

  • Component kitting
  • Spares and service packs
  • Grade colour-coding
